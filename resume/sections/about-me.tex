\section{Research Interests}

    \begin{onecolentry}
        PhD candidate in \textbf{software testing}. I develop testing techniques that reveal bugs in complex software, where inputs must satisfy many constraints and there is often no simple way to check an output. I have reported \textbf{323} bugs (\textbf{236} confirmed) in \textbf{11} systems from \textbf{5} domains: deep learning libraries, LLM serving engines, databases, compilers, and system software~\href{https://abidnazirisami.github.io/bugs/}{[list]}. My dissertation focuses on deep learning libraries (\textbf{PyTorch}, \textbf{TensorFlow}, \textbf{JAX}).
    \end{onecolentry}
